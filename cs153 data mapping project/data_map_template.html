About Data Mapper

Data Mapper helps you visualize location-based data and turn it into action. Use it for advocacy, grant proposals, research, or teaching.

Features: Start in the Data tab to discover datasets or paste CSVs. Use Settings -> Layers to toggle stacked datasets, colors, and patterns. Export PNG, CSV, or Save PDF (print dialog). Insights -> Ask gives instant stats; add an OpenAI key for AI text when the map is served over http://127.0.0.1 (local /dm-openai relay).

Share your map to advocate for change or cite it in reports and proposals.
