About

Common Ground Atlas

A studio for turning open data into maps that make inequality visible, so students, advocates, NGOs and policymakers can see where help is needed most.

Find data

Federated search across the World Bank (1,500 indicators), Our World in Data (7,000+ charts), the Humanitarian Data Exchange and Wikipedia, plus 30 curated datasets organized by the UN Sustainable Development Goals.

Bring data

Upload CSV, Excel or JSON, paste from a spreadsheet, or scrape any public web page. Places are matched automatically: 250 countries and 4,500+ states/provinces in many spellings.

See it

3D globe with extruded values and pulsing priority areas, flat and 3D maps, bivariate color, heatmaps, and an animated timeline.

Act on it

Benchmarks with people-affected counts, auto-written findings, rankings, correlations, poster PNGs and printable impact briefs.
